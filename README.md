# Determination: An Undertale mod

Determination is a mod for Rimworld that adds features and concepts from Undertale.

## Features:
- **Souls:** Every humanoid pawn is assigned a soul type on generation, and each soul type can have multiple variants. These offer minor stat buffs.
    - **Soullessness:** When a pawn is brought back from death without a soul, they gain a special status. They will have the psychopath trait and require regular injections of determination to survive.
- **LV:** Killing enemies grants EXP to the pawn who dealt the final blow. Gather enough EXP and they can level up, offering more powerful stat bonuses.
- **Determination:** A stat derived from the mental break threshold of a pawn. Allows pawns to self revive upon death.
- **Monsters:** Most xenohumans are classified as monsters, and are assigned a special soul. Monsters have highly efficient metabolisms, but are weak to determined human attackers.

## Compatibility:
NOT safe to add to existing saves.
NOT safe to remove from existing saves.
